ℹ️ About CloudTasks

🎯 Focused Task Management
CloudTasks helps you stay focused by showing you just a few tasks at a time. Complete tasks to earn rewards and see new challenges when your partner earns rewards too.
💾 Cloud Storage
All your tasks, goals, and rewards are stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore, so they're always available across devices.
🔒 Secure & Private
Each user's data is completely separate and secure. Your family members can't see your tasks, and you can't see theirs.
